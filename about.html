A higher perspective.

Latitude Stone is a North Texas commercial real estate advisory and brokerage firm built to give clients experienced guidance without the layers that can come with larger firms.

We stay close to the work, communicate directly, and approach each assignment with a long-term view of the client, the opportunity, and the decision in front of us.

A different kind of brokerage.

Independent by design and personal by nature. Our size keeps us close to the people and decisions that matter, while our experience brings perspective to every assignment.
