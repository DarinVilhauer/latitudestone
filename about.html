Meet Darin

After more than 20 years in leadership and commercial real estate, Darin founded Latitude Stone to build the kind of brokerage he would want to work with: responsive, straightforward, and focused on the long term.

He works with investors, property owners, developers, and businesses across North Texas. His approach is simple: understand the objective, be candid about the options, and stay involved through execution.

North Texas is also home. Darin and his wife, Michelle, are building Latitude Stone with a long-term view of the business and the community it serves.
