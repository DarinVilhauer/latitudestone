Latitude Stone.

Commercial real estate advice built around relationships,

A higher perspective.

Latitude Stone is a North Texas commercial real estate advisory and brokerage firm focused on practical advice, personal service, and lasting client relationships.

We stay close to the work, communicate directly, and approach each assignment with a strong understanding of the client, the opportunity, and the decision in front of us.

A different kind of brokerage.

Independent by design and personal by nature. Our size keeps us close to the people and decisions that matter, while our experience brings perspective to every assignment.

Our focus

The business model is simple: client first.

Whether you have years of commercial real estate experience or are just getting started, Latitude Stone meets you where you are. We help you understand the market, evaluate your options, and make informed decisions with greater clarity.

Our leadership

Darin Vilhauer CEO

After more than 20 years in leadership and commercial real estate, Darin founded Latitude Stone to create a brokerage that is responsive, straightforward, and focused on lasting relationships.

He works with investors, property owners, developers, and businesses across North Texas, bringing experience, perspective, and a hands-on approach to every relationship.

North Texas is also home. Darin and his wife, Michelle, are growing Latitude Stone with a lasting commitment to the business and the community it serves.
