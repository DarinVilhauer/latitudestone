Latitude Stone Homepage v1.9 – Monochrome Design Study

Changes included:
- Removed gold from the interface in favor of black, white, and gray tones.
- Transparent header retained; active navigation and outlined buttons are now white/gray.
- Hero headline reduced from bold to regular/medium visual weight.
- Section headlines use the same modern sans-serif visual system.
- Service titles are slightly stronger than the body copy.
- What We Do cards changed from dark blue to charcoal gray.
- Our Market and Market Intelligence backgrounds changed from cream to white.
- Our Market satellite image changed to a soft white/gray monochrome treatment.
- Dividers, labels, footer accents, metrics, and hover states changed from gold to neutral gray.
- Existing page content, layout, photos, and section order preserved.
